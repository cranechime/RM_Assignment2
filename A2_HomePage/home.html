Our mission

Here at Sea Turtles Worldwide, we are most passionate about ensuring that sea turtles around the globe are able to thrive in the wild.
All of our earnings are put towards this goal of ours which we strive to accomplish by:

Rehabilitating
injured turtles

Cleaning up sea
turtle habitats

Restoring
ecosystems
