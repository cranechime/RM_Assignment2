The client, named Kaylyn, owns a company that protects the habitats and lives of sea turtles and wants to build a website to receive public donations. She expresses that her business is greatly struggling at the moment. This is because of the recent buildup of plastic waste in many sea turtle habitats and the rise in temperatures, which has been killing many coral reefs. Thus, her team’s jobs have become much more taxing and it is urgent that she receives the most donations possible with this new site, or else her company and passion project risk crumbling apart.

Only small changes were made to the design of the site. This includes the header and footer, including a new logo, as the original layout was satisfactory and easy to implement.

No AI was used.
